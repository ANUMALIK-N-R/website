// 01 — About the Lab

At the Frontier of
Molecular Intelligence

The AIMS Lab (AI for Molecular Sciences) at Digital University Kerala is an interdisciplinary research group working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and chemical biology. We develop and apply computational methods to accelerate molecular design, drug discovery, and biomarker identification.

Our team combines expertise in synthetic organic chemistry, deep learning, bioinformatics, and pharmacology to address complex challenges in human health — from multi-target drug design to next-generation theranostic agents.

Housed in the School of Digital Sciences, AIMS Lab brings together the quantitative rigour of data science with the experimental creativity of molecular biology to generate real-world impact.

Work With Us
AI-Driven Molecular Design
Deep learning models for molecular property prediction, generative design, and synthesis planning.
Experimental Validation
Bridging in-silico predictions with wet-lab synthesis to confirm and iterate on computational findings.
Multi-Omics Integration
Data fusion across genomics, transcriptomics, and metabolomics for disease biomarker discovery.
Translational Impact
From computational insight to viable therapeutic leads with measurable clinical relevance.
// 02 — Research Areas

Five Pillars
of Discovery

Our research spans five interconnected domains, united by the common goal of using AI responsibly and rigorously to advance molecular sciences.

01
AI-Driven Molecular Modelling and Synthesis
Generative and predictive AI models to design novel molecular scaffolds, optimise synthetic routes, and accelerate hit-to-lead campaigns in drug discovery.
Deep LearningGenerative AIRetrosynthesis
02
Graph Neural Networks for Molecular Property Prediction
GNN architectures to encode molecular graphs and predict ADMET properties, bioactivity profiles, and toxicity endpoints with high accuracy and interpretability.
GNNsADMETCheminformatics
03
Organo-Fluorophores for Theranostic Applications
Designing and synthesising novel organic fluorescent probes that combine diagnostic imaging with targeted therapy, enabling simultaneous detection and treatment of disease.
FluorescenceTheranosticsImaging
04
OMICS and Integrative Data Analysis for Biomarker Discovery
Integrating genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics using machine learning to identify disease biomarkers and novel therapeutic targets.
GenomicsProteomicsBiomarkers
05
Network Pharmacology for Multi-Target Drug Discovery
Building drug-target interaction networks to identify polypharmacology opportunities and design multi-target agents for complex, multi-factorial diseases.
Network AnalysisPolypharmacologySystems Biology
